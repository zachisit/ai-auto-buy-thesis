## Lessons / Observations
1. **NEW RULE: All positions must have limit sell orders at target from day one.** The ET exit at $19.82 required the operator to be watching the chart and manually sell. This is not how the experiment should run. Going forward: buy stop to enter, stop loss to protect, limit sell to take profit. Fully self-managing.

2. **NEW RULE: Minimum stop distance of 1.5 ATRs from entry.** LYFT's stop at 1.0 ATR was triggered by normal daily volatility on day 2, before the trade had any chance to work. AAL's stop at 1.0 ATR had the same problem. Future trades need wider stops to survive normal noise. If wider stops mean fewer shares (to stay within risk budget), that's acceptable.

3. **Airlines/travel are not tradeable in this environment.** Three attempts (DAL, AAL, LYFT), three losses, -$148.50 total. Oil above $100 is a structural headwind that earnings beats, ceasefire hopes, and rideshare diversification cannot overcome. The experiment should not enter any airline, cruise, or rideshare position while oil remains above $90.

4. **Energy is the experiment's proven edge.** DVN (+$72.60) and ET (+$46.11) = +$118.71 from two trades. Both used the same playbook: midstream/E&P in an elevated oil environment, $20-50 price range for good share count, tight ATR, POC support, patient holding through VSA signals. The next trade should start with energy screening.

5. **"Diversification" between correlated sectors is fake diversification.** AAL (airline) and LYFT (rideshare) stopped out on the same day. Both are consumer/travel names impacted by oil prices and consumer sentiment. Holding both felt diversified but wasn't — they're the same trade. Real diversification would be energy + a sector that moves independently (tech, financials, utilities).

6. **The new operating procedure changes everything.** Limit sell orders at target would have captured ET's profit automatically. The operator shouldn't need to watch charts. This aligns the experiment with its original design: LLM makes decisions at 8am, operator executes orders, positions self-manage throughout the day.
