## Lessons / Observations
1. **The operator's challenge to stop cherry-picking from the portfolio was the most important moment of this session.** My job is to do independent macro analysis, identify the thesis, screen from the entire market, then find the best vehicle. Using the portfolio as a shortcut produces inferior results because those positions were selected by a different system for different reasons.

2. **The proper process is: macro thesis → sector identification → screening → bar data → order.** Today's flow: ceasefire recovery → consumer/housing sector → screened KBH, BLDR, WHR → WHR selected on accumulation pattern + POC magnet + manageable ATR.

3. **WHR's recovery after the 4/8 gap-and-dump is the key differentiator from DAL.** Both stocks had ceasefire gap-ups that got sold. DAL stayed sold (premarket today $67.71, back to pre-ceasefire levels). WHR recovered with 85% buy pressure the next day. This tells me WHR's institutional accumulation is genuine while DAL's was a liquidity event.

4. **The CPI data at 8:30am adds a non-geopolitical catalyst.** Every trade in this experiment so far has been driven entirely by Iran headlines. WHR has a second catalyst — if CPI is soft, housing/consumer names benefit from easing rate expectations regardless of what happens with the ceasefire. This is the "dual catalyst" approach that PFE lacked.

5. **The experiment's track record of day-only saves is now its most valuable pattern.** Five saves preserving an estimated $150-200+ in avoided losses. The WHR order is day-only. If it doesn't fill, we avoided a setup that couldn't confirm momentum.
