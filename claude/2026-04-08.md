The likely sequence: market sold defensives hard as capital rotated into risk-on plays ahead of/following the ceasefire news. PFE was caught in the defensive-to-cyclical rotation — the exact scenario where defensive stocks underperform.

**Key lesson:** Defensive positioning preserves capital during crisis but becomes a liability at the moment of resolution. PFE did its job for 6 days but the $26.55 stop was too far below entry ($1.72 gap) for a stock with only $0.64 ATR. The risk/reward was sub-1:1 from the start. Future defensive plays need tighter stops or should be sized smaller to limit max loss.

**Audit answers:**
