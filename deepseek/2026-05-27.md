## Decision Rationale

The macro environment has become decidedly more bullish, driven by the sharp drop in oil prices. However, this move is purely news-driven. The VSA framework requires us to enter on technical patterns like a "spring" or a low-volume "test," not on a headline gap.

- **Airlines:** While the drop in oil is a direct benefit, the airline stocks have already rallied sharply over the past week without any significant pullback. They are now gapping higher again this morning on the oil news, which creates a poor risk/reward for entry. Without a low-volume "no supply" bar to confirm that sellers have been exhausted, chasing this move would violate our primary trading rule.

- **Retailers:** Several retailers are gapping on earnings or oil optimism. This introduces event risk, as the market's reaction to a report can be unpredictable and override any technical pattern.

- **Tech:** The AI-driven rally in semiconductor stocks is the dominant theme. However, these stocks, such as Micron (MU), are extremely extended and trading at valuations that are incompatible with our VSA discipline. There is no spring or test in sight.

With no clear VSA setups and a market moving on macroeconomic headlines, the only disciplined course of action is to remain in cash and wait for a clear technical pattern to emerge. We will continue to monitor for pullbacks and low-volume tests in sectors that have been beaten down.
